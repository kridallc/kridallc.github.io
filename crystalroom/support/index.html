Frequently Asked Questions

What hardware do I need to use CrystalRoom?
CrystalRoom is built exclusively for Apple Vision Pro running visionOS. It is not available on iPhone, iPad, or Mac. You need an Apple Vision Pro headset to use the app.
What can I do in CrystalRoom?
Explore from your space — rotate, zoom, inspect atoms, build supercells, highlight elements, and arrange windows around you, with undo/redo and session restore (your structure, supercell, and view come back when you return).
Step inside the crystal — enter full immersion, walk through a room-scale lattice, and tap atoms around you to inspect them.
Measure atomic geometry — two atoms for distance, three for an angle, with guide lines on the structure and a measurement history.
See more of the structure — independent a/b/c supercells, coordination polyhedra, edge atoms, Miller planes with live d-spacing, element search, and fractional/Cartesian coordinates.
Guided exploration — a 20-step workspace tour, an immersive Halite tour, and a “Tour This Structure” experience that adapts to the crystal you are viewing.
Built on real crystallographic data — structures reconstructed from bundled CIF data, backed by an automated scientific test suite.
What is Immersive Space mode?
Immersive Space mode uses Apple Vision Pro's spatial computing capabilities to place your crystal structure in your physical environment at room scale. You can literally walk around a unit cell. Enable it from the toolbar inside the app. You can exit back to windowed mode at any time.
How do I measure bond distances and angles?
Tap any atom in the structure to select it, then tap a second atom. CrystalRoom will display the bond distance between them. For angles, select three atoms in sequence — the angle at the middle atom is calculated automatically.
What is a supercell and how do I expand it?
A supercell is a repeating tile of the unit cell — expanding from 1×1×1 to larger grids lets you see the full periodic lattice and how structures propagate through space. Supercell expansion from 1×1×1 up to 5×5×5; the largest unit cells (corundum, calcite, zircon, forsterite) are capped at 3×3×3–4×4×4 to keep Vision Pro responsive.
What is CIF Import and how do I use it?
CIF (Crystallographic Information File) is the universal standard for sharing crystal structure data, and many structures are freely available from the Crystallography Open Database. CIF Import is available through institutional licensing — if you're at a university, research lab, or educational program interested in deploying CrystalRoom with CIF Import, contact us at support_crystalroom@kridallc.com.
How do I use Auto-Spin for a presentation or demo?
Auto-Spin continuously rotates the structure at a gentle pace — ideal for demonstrations, lectures, or leaving a structure on display. Enable it from the toolbar. You can still zoom and adjust the supercell size while auto-spin is active.
How do I request a refund?
All purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. To request a refund, visit reportaproblem.apple.com or contact Apple Support — we do not have access to your payment information.
Where do the built-in crystal structures come from?
The eleven built-in structures — Corundum (Al₂O₃), Calcite (CaCO₃), Fluorite (CaF₂), Zircon (ZrSiO₄), Forsterite (Mg₂SiO₄), Diamond (C), Gold (Au), Halite (NaCl), Barium titanate (BaTiO₃, a tetragonal perovskite), Quartz (SiO₂), and Rutile (TiO₂) — are reconstructed from CIF crystallographic data bundled directly with the app. No internet connection is required to view them.
Why is 5×5×5 greyed out for some structures?
Corundum, calcite, zircon and forsterite have large unit cells. Above a few thousand atoms Apple Vision Pro cannot render a windowed scene smoothly, so CrystalRoom caps those structures at 3×3×3 or 4×4×4. The supercell panel shows the atom count and the limit. The seven compact structures go all the way to 5×5×5.
My windows disappeared when I entered immersion.
That is intentional. Entering the immersive space closes the app’s windows so nothing floats in front of the lattice. Tap Exit on the head-anchored toolbar and the windows come back exactly as you left them.
I reopened the app and only the main window, axis gizmo and legend appeared.
Also intentional. Reopening CrystalRoom — after closing its main window, a force quit, or a restart — restores your structure, supercell and view, but always starts with the default window pair so the layout is predictable. Only an immersive round trip brings back every window you had open.
I closed every panel but one, then closed the main window, and that panel stayed.
visionOS does not let an app close its own last window. Close that panel yourself; the next tap on the app icon opens the main window as usual.
Does CrystalRoom collect any data?
No. See the privacy policy — no accounts, no analytics, nothing leaves the device.
