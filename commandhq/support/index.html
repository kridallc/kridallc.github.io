Getting started
What’s the fastest way to learn the app?

Choose “Explore with the guided tour” when you first open CommandHQ, or later choose Restore Sample Project (in the menu bar on the Mac, in Settings on iPhone and iPad, or from ⌘K). The sample project has realistic data in every view. Many features in the in-app guide have a short “Watch” video, in English.

Do I need a Mac?

No. CommandHQ is complete on an iPhone (including iPhone Duo, folded or open) or an iPad on its own, and on a Mac. Apple Watch works with the iPhone app. A few things only make sense on a Mac and live there: Autolog, local AI with Ollama, recording both sides of a call, the menu-bar timer and the ⌃⌥⌘Space hotkey.

Is there a Windows, Android or web version?

No. CommandHQ is for Apple devices only: iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch. There is no Windows, Android or web app, and no plans for one today.

Can I keep everything on one device?

Yes. iCloud Sync is a switch in Settings on each device. With it off, your projects stay only on that device; you can still back up to a folder you choose. If you use CommandHQ for work, check your employer’s rules on where work data may be stored.

Can I import my tasks from another app?

Yes. CommandHQ imports CSV, TaskPaper and Apple Reminders, including Todoist project exports, so your tasks come across from most apps. The Library also imports references from Zotero, EndNote, BibTeX and RIS.

What keyboard shortcuts should I know on the Mac?
  • ⌃⌥⌘Space: Quick Entry from any app
  • ⌘K: jump to any project, task, view or setting
  • ⌘1 to ⌘5: Dashboard, Kanban, Gantt, Time, Canvas
  • ⌘T: Today
Which languages does it speak?

12: English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Dutch, Polish, Portuguese (Brazil), Japanese, Korean, and Chinese (Simplified and Traditional). CommandHQ follows your device’s language, or you can choose one in Settings.

Forecasts
How are forecasts calculated?

Forecasts are based on the work you actually finish, and they update as you work. They’re calculated on your device, and nothing leaves it. A forecast is an estimate, not a guarantee.

How accurate are forecasts?

Forecasts are estimates based on the work you actually finish, and they update as you work. We tested them against 30,000+ finished milestones from over a million tasks, drawn from external sources. They can’t know about scope changes, holidays, or a project you stop working on, so treat them as an early warning, not a guarantee.

Sync, data and privacy
How do I sync between Mac, iPhone and iPad?

Sign in to the same Apple Account on each device with iCloud Drive turned on, then switch on iCloud Sync in CommandHQ’s Settings on each device (it is off for a new install on iPhone and iPad). Use Sync Now to check at any time. Apple Watch syncs through your iPhone.

A change isn’t showing on my other device.
  • Check that both devices use the same Apple Account and that iCloud Drive has space.
  • Open CommandHQ on both devices and press Sync Now (⌘R on the Mac).
  • Low Power Mode and poor connections can delay iCloud. If it is still stuck after a few minutes, email us with both devices’ versions.
Does it work offline?

Yes. Your projects are files on your device, so you can plan, track time and take notes with no connection. Changes sync when you’re back online. AI from Anthropic or OpenAI needs a connection; on-device Apple Intelligence doesn’t.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no CommandHQ account or company server. Your projects live in plain files and sync through your own iCloud.

Is my data encrypted?

Your projects are stored in your iCloud Drive, which Apple encrypts in transit and on its servers. If you turn on Advanced Data Protection for your Apple Account, iCloud Drive is end-to-end encrypted, so only your devices can read it.

How do I back up or restore?

CommandHQ keeps automatic snapshots and can also write backups to a folder you choose (Backup in Settings). Restore shows a preview first and keeps a safety copy of your current data, labelled with the date, before it changes anything.

Can I get my data out?

Always. Projects are plain, readable files, and you can export CSV (free), PDF and HTML reports, timesheets and canvases as PNG or PDF.

What if CommandHQ ever stops?

You keep everything. Projects are plain, readable files in your own iCloud, and you can export CSV, PDF and HTML at any time. The app keeps working on your devices as installed.

Purchases
I bought Pro but a device still shows the free plan.

Open Settings › CommandHQ Pro › Restore Purchases on that device, signed in with the Apple Account you bought with. One purchase covers Mac, iPhone, iPad and Watch. Family Sharing works on Yearly and Lifetime.

How do I cancel or get a refund?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: on iPhone or iPad go to Settings › your name › Subscriptions; on the Mac open the App Store › your account › Account Settings › Subscriptions. Refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

AI
How do I turn on AI?

Open Settings › AI and pick a provider: Apple Intelligence (on supported devices, no setup), Ollama (you install Ollama from ollama.com and download a model yourself; CommandHQ only connects to it, and each model’s license is yours to follow), or Anthropic or OpenAI with your own API key. AI features are part of Pro.

Does CommandHQ charge for AI or see my prompts?

No. CommandHQ runs no AI service. Requests go from your device to the provider you chose, and any API charges are between you and that provider. AI Settings shows what gets sent.

Recording
Do I need permission to record a meeting or lecture?

Often, yes. Recording laws differ by country and by US state, and many places require every participant’s consent. Always tell people you are recording and get their permission before you start; it is your responsibility, as our Terms explain. Recordings and transcripts stay on your devices and in your iCloud; we never receive them.

Where does transcription happen?

On your device, using Apple’s speech recognition, in the languages your device supports. Nothing is sent to us. The one exception is your choice: if you choose OpenAI as your AI and a device can’t transcribe a voice note itself, CommandHQ can send that recording to OpenAI, only after asking your permission.

Time and calendar
Why can’t I see my meetings?

CommandHQ needs Calendar access to show meetings beside planned work. Turn it on in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Calendars (Mac) or Settings › Privacy & Security › Calendars (iPhone and iPad).

Can I see my Google or Outlook calendar?

Yes, if you add that account to Apple’s Calendar on your device. CommandHQ shows every calendar the Calendar app has, beside your planned work.

What is Autolog?

A Mac-only option that notes which app is in front so the end-of-day Reconcile sheet can suggest time sessions you forgot to track. It never records your screen, never syncs and never writes anything until you confirm.

Teams
Can my team use CommandHQ together?

Not today. CommandHQ is built for one person running several projects: freelancers, founders, project leads keeping their own plan, researchers, students and makers. It has no shared workspaces, comments or assignees. We may offer a team version in the future. If we do, it will be a separate product with its own pricing, and it won’t be included in Lifetime or the current Pro plans. Everything you have for one person, including every future Pro update, stays yours.
