Privacy Policy

CommandHQ · Developed by Krida LLC · Effective date: November 17, 2026

The short version

  • The CommandHQ app collects no data. Its App Store privacy label is “Data Not Collected”.
  • No account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no server that receives your data. Our only server sends out the in-app guide videos.
  • Your projects are files on your devices and, if you turn on sync, in your own iCloud. We cannot see them.
  • AI is optional. On-device AI stays on your device. If you add your own Anthropic or OpenAI key, what you send goes to that provider, under their policy. We never train AI on your data.
  • If you record meetings, the recordings and transcripts stay with you, unless you choose to send a voice note to OpenAI for transcription (see AI features). Getting participants’ consent is your responsibility.
  • We don’t sell or share personal information.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details.
  • This website uses Google Analytics. In the EU and UK it runs only if you accept. Change your choice any time in Cookie settings.

Definitions

  • App: CommandHQ for macOS, iOS, iPadOS and watchOS, including its widgets and extensions.
  • We, us, our: Krida LLC, Golden, Colorado, U.S.A. For the GDPR, Krida LLC is the data controller for this Website.
  • Personal Data: information that identifies, or can identify, a person.
  • Website: kridallc.com/commandhq and its subpages.
Part 1 · The CommandHQ app

What the app collects

Nothing. CommandHQ does not transmit Personal Data or usage data to us or to any partner of ours. It contains no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs, and there is no CommandHQ account. No server of ours receives your data; the only one, videos.kridallc.com, sends out guide videos (see below).

Where your data lives

Your projects, tasks, canvases, Library items, recordings and settings are stored on your device. Project data is kept as plain, readable files.

  • iCloud sync (optional): when you turn it on, the app stores and syncs your data in your own iCloud account using Apple’s iCloud services. Apple handles that data under Apple’s Privacy Policy. We have no access to it.
  • Backups and snapshots: stay on your device, or in a folder you choose.
  • Apple Watch: receives a snapshot from your iPhone and sends your actions back to it.

AI features (optional)

AI features run only when you open one or ask a question. You choose the provider:

  • Apple Intelligence: runs on your device where available.
  • Ollama: runs locally on your Mac, if you have installed it yourself. CommandHQ only connects to the Ollama server you run; Ollama and the models you choose are separate software with their own terms. If you point CommandHQ at an Ollama server on another computer, your requests go to that computer.
  • Anthropic or OpenAI with your own API key: the text you ask about (for example a task list or a project description) is sent directly from your device to that provider. The first time, the app names the provider and asks your permission; nothing is sent until you allow it, and you can revoke it in AI Settings. If you choose OpenAI, a voice note can also be sent to OpenAI for transcription (on devices that can’t transcribe it themselves); the recording itself then goes to OpenAI, again only after you allow it. We are not involved, and that provider’s privacy policy applies. Your key is stored in your Keychain (synced with iCloud Keychain only if you turn that on). AI Settings in the app shows what gets sent.

Device permissions

The app asks for a permission only when you use the feature that needs it, and you can change it at any time in your device’s Settings:

  • Calendars and Reminders: to show meetings beside planned work, publish planned blocks to a “CommandHQ” calendar if you choose, and import or send reminders you pick. The app never deletes anything in Reminders.
  • Microphone, audio capture and speech recognition: to record voice notes and meetings. Transcription runs on your device, unless you choose OpenAI transcription for a voice note.
  • Camera and photos: to add pictures, scans and video to canvases and the Library.

Autolog (Mac only, optional) notes which app is in front so it can suggest time sessions at the end of the day. It doesn’t record your screen, stays on your Mac, never syncs, and never writes anything without your confirmation.

Recordings and other people’s information

Recordings, transcripts and anything else you capture about other people (meeting participants, clients, contacts in your tasks) stay on your devices and in your own iCloud. We never receive them. For that information you, not Krida, decide how it is collected and used. Under laws such as the GDPR that makes you its controller, and you are responsible for giving any notice and getting any consent the law requires before you record. See Recording and transcription in our Terms.

No AI training

Krida never uses your data to train AI models. We don’t receive it. If you use your own Anthropic or OpenAI key, that provider’s policy decides whether it may use your requests. Check its settings.

Network requests the app makes for you

  • Look Up Details (Library, off by default): when you turn it on and agree, the app sends a DOI to Crossref (api.crossref.org) or an ISBN to Open Library (openlibrary.org) to fill in a reference’s details.
  • Link previews: when you add a link, the app may load that web page to show its title and image, as Safari would.
  • AI providers you configured yourself (see above).
  • Guide videos: the in-app guide downloads a list of its videos from videos.kridallc.com at most once a day, and only when the guide shows an entry, never at launch. When you tap Watch, the app streams that one video from the same address; nothing loads before the tap. These requests carry no identifier, account, cookie or app data. The videos are stored with Cloudflare (R2), which only sends files out and receives nothing from the app. Like any web server, Cloudflare sees your IP address and the request in order to deliver the video; we don’t use the video server to identify anyone. Offline, or when no list is available, there is no Watch button.

Purchases

CommandHQ Pro is sold through Apple’s App Store. Apple processes payments and manages subscriptions. Your device checks your purchase with Apple directly; we receive no payment information. We receive only the aggregated sales reports Apple gives every developer.

Crash reports

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may give us anonymous crash reports and diagnostics. You control this in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

When you email us

If you contact support, we receive your email address and whatever you send, such as screenshots. We use it only to reply, and delete it when it is no longer needed.

Part 2 · This website

This Website

The Website is separate from the app: nothing you do in the app is linked to your visits here.

Hosting

The Website is hosted on GitHub Pages and may use Cloudflare to deliver media; the videos on the Website are delivered by Cloudflare from videos.kridallc.com. These providers receive your IP address and browser details to deliver pages and protect against abuse.

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to see which pages are viewed, the approximate region of visitors and the type of device and browser, and how the site is used: which guide videos are played and how far, the words typed into the video search, which FAQ questions are opened, and which App Store and social media buttons are clicked. None of this identifies you, and none of it is linked to the app. It sets first-party cookies named _ga and _ga_*. We do not use analytics for advertising, and advertising storage is disabled.

  • EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland: analytics runs only after you choose Accept.
  • Elsewhere: analytics is on by default and you can turn it off.
  • Change your choice any time with Cookie settings, linked in every page’s footer.

Google LLC processes this data for us and is certified under the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework and its UK and Swiss extensions. See how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Part 3 · Both

Your rights

Because the app sends us no Personal Data, there is no app data for us to access, correct or delete; you control all of it on your devices and in your iCloud. For the Website, our legal basis for analytics in the EEA, UK and Switzerland is consent, which you can withdraw at any time. You may ask about or ask us to delete data from your visits by emailing us, and you may complain to your local data protection authority.

US state privacy rights

We do not sell or share personal information, or use it for targeted advertising, as those terms are defined in California’s CCPA/CPRA and similar US state laws. The App collects no personal information. For the Website, you can opt out of analytics at any time in Cookie settings, and our site honors Global Privacy Control signals by treating them as an opt-out. You may ask us what Website data relates to you, or ask us to delete it, and we won’t treat you differently for asking.

International users

Krida is based in the United States. Website analytics may be processed in the US by Google under the safeguards described above. App data is stored on your devices and in iCloud under Apple’s terms, not by us.

Children

CommandHQ is rated 4+ and collects no data from anyone, of any age. The Website is not directed at children.

Changes

We will post any change to this policy at this address with a new effective date. If we ever add a feature that changes what data leaves your device, we will update this policy and the App Store privacy label before that feature ships.
