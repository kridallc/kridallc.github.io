Who it's for

Built for one person running many projects.

If your week is several projects with real deadlines and nobody else keeping track, this is for you.

Freelancers and consultants

  • Timers, billable rates and timesheets (CSV)
  • Timesheets export to your invoicing tool; CommandHQ doesn’t send invoices itself
  • Client-ready status reports as PDF or HTML
  • Planned vs actual hours, per project

Founders and indie makers

  • Every product on one roadmap and portfolio
  • Milestone forecasts before a launch slips
  • Quick capture from any app, and one search box for everything

Researchers and students

  • A Library for papers, with Zotero, EndNote, BibTeX and RIS import
  • Live Notes: record a lecture, get an on-device transcript
  • Thesis, grant and course milestones on a timeline (Gantt)

Anyone juggling several projects

  • A home renovation, a side project and a job, side by side
  • A Daily Briefing of what's overdue and at risk
  • Works on just an iPhone or iPad

Built for one person today: no shared workspaces, comments or assignees. A team version may come later as a separate product; it wouldn’t be part of Lifetime or current Pro plans. More about teams →

Switching takes minutes

Bring your tasks with you.

Import a Todoist project export, Apple Reminders, any CSV or TaskPaper file, and references from Zotero, EndNote, BibTeX or RIS. Leaving is just as easy: your projects are plain files, with CSV, PDF and HTML export.

Not a companion app

The whole app, on whatever you have.

CommandHQ isn't a Mac app with a phone add-on. An iPhone or an iPad on its own runs the full app: timelines (Gantt), forecasts, canvases (whiteboards), Live Notes, timers, the Library and AI. CommandHQ is for Apple devices only: there’s no Windows, Android or web version. Add more devices whenever you like, and they stay in step through your own iCloud.

  • Every view on iPhone and iPad; a few extras, such as Autolog, are Mac-only
  • iPhone Duo: the layout opens up to the big inner screen, with every view at full size
  • Apple Pencil on iPad: handwriting, ink to text, sketches
  • Live Activity and Dynamic Island for the running timer
  • Apple Watch: today's tasks, timers, complications, and a warning when a milestone this week is likely to be late
  • Home Screen, Lock Screen and desktop widgets
Accessibility

Usable by everyone, tested on real devices.

Screens were audited on Mac, iPad and iPhone in several languages, not just in theory.

  • VoiceOver: controls named, the Gantt read row by row
  • Voice Control on Mac, iPad and iPhone
  • Larger Text on iPhone and iPad
  • Dark Mode and Reduced Motion
  • Sufficient contrast
  • Never colour alone: status, priority and lateness also use shapes, words and symbols

The whole app, on any one device.

iPhone, iPhone Duo, iPad or Mac on its own. A few extras are Mac-only, such as Autolog and the menu-bar timer. Free with one project.
