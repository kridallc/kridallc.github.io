FAQ

Questions about buying

Plans and purchases

What’s free, and what needs Pro?

Free gives you the Inbox plus one project with unlimited tasks, List, Kanban and calendar views, deadline forecasts, the Library, reminders, iCloud sync, widgets and the Apple Watch app; canvases can be viewed, presented and exported. Pro adds unlimited projects, Gantt and saved plans, one-click Replan, time reports and timesheets, canvas editing, the PDF reader, sources and citations, templates, status reports, PDF export and AI.

Is there a free trial?

New subscribers get one 14-day free trial, on Monthly or Yearly. Lifetime has no trial, but the Free plan lets you use CommandHQ with one project for as long as you like before you decide.

Does one purchase cover all my devices?

Yes. Pro or Lifetime unlocks CommandHQ on every iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch signed in to the same Apple Account. Family Sharing works on Yearly and Lifetime.

What exactly does Lifetime include?

Every Pro feature of CommandHQ, including future Pro updates, on all your devices, paid once. It doesn’t include separately sold apps, a possible future team product, services that would run on a server, or what third-party AI providers charge you.

How does the launch price work?

Lifetime is $69.99 until December 15, 2026, then $99.99. Buy it during that time and it’s yours for good at $69.99.

I subscribed. Can I switch to Lifetime?

Yes. Buy Lifetime in Settings › CommandHQ Pro, then cancel your subscription in your Apple Account so it doesn’t renew. Apple handles any refund request at reportaproblem.apple.com.

What happens if my subscription ends?

Nothing is hidden or deleted. Your Inbox and one project you choose stay editable; the rest stay readable, searchable and exportable, and you can still complete tasks. Resubscribe and everything unlocks again.

Teams

Is it for teams?

Not today. CommandHQ is built for one person running several projects: freelancers, founders, project leads keeping their own plan, researchers, students and makers. It has no shared workspaces, comments or assignees. We may offer a team version in the future. If we do, it will be a separate product with its own pricing, and it won’t be included in Lifetime or the current Pro plans. Everything you have for one person, including every future Pro update, stays yours.
